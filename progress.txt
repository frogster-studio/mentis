## Item 3 — two-choice pick beside `squareChoices` (done)
- answer-matching: `quickHomeQuestionChoices(question, rng): [string, string]` in shuffle.ts, takes `ShuffleableQuestion`. 1st rng call picks the wrong choice (`floor(rng*len)`), 2nd is the coin flip (`< 0.5` → Canonical Answer left). Tuple return so callers get exactly two without asserting.
- why not reuse `squareChoices`: PRD rules it out (Canonical Answer right 3 in 4).
- spec: `scriptedRng(...values)` helper in shuffle.spec.ts; each wrong choice reachable (0/0.4/0.99), both orders (0.49/0.5), no mutation, 20 seeds always Canonical + one wrong.
- files: packages/answer-matching/src/shuffle{,.spec}.ts
