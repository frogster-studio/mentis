## Item 4 — mobile: Anecdote text segments
Done: `anecdoteTextSegments(text)` → `{ text, emphasis: "regular" | "bold" | "italic" }[]`, one regex `/(\*\*|__)([\s\S]+?)\1/g`: backreference closes a span on its own marker only, so the other marker inside stays literal (no nesting) and an orphan never matches (literal). `[\s\S]` lets a span cross a line break; breaks stay in their segment. Empty span (`****`) stays literal (`+?` needs 1 char). Empty text → `[]`.
Decisions: emphasis as a local string union, like mobile's `status`/`layout` unions; the contracts-enum rule is for shared vocabularies, this one is mobile render-only. File `features/anecdote/text-segments.ts`; the card item maps emphasis to `TEXT.anecdote`/`anecdoteBold`/`anecdoteItalic`.
