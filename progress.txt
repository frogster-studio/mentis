## PRD-2 Item 1 — /mentis/delete-account (web) — DONE

Done: the sixth page of the site, the Play Console « Delete account URL » target, with both deletion paths.
Decisions:
- routes.ts gains deleteAccount: "/mentis/delete-account"; routes.test.ts asserts the six paths and that the new one stays under /mentis. SiteFooter untouched — Google wants the URL to land on the page itself, and the four legal links are enough.
- src/lib/delete-account.ts is pure TS (no JSX, so vitest reaches it in the node environment): IN_APP_STEPS, DELETION_EMAIL_SUBJECT, DELETION_EMAIL_BODY_LINES, DELETION_EMAIL_BODY (the lines joined by \n) and buildDeletionMailto(address).
- buildDeletionMailto uses encodeURIComponent, not URLSearchParams: the latter encodes a space as "+", which several mail clients paste literally into the subject.
- The body is kept as lines, not one blob: the page renders one <p> per line and the spec asserts the six lines and the three identity fields separately.
- copy-button.tsx is the site's first and only "use client" component. It guards on navigator.clipboard being absent and simply does nothing then — the address is also a mailto: link and the template is displayed in full, so nothing is lost.
- The page names Mentis and PUBLISHER.tradeName, says the deletion is immediate and irreversible with no retention, lists what is erased (account, pseudo, scores, sessions, competition attempts and answers, standings, the Premium mirror) and what is not ours (the purchase record at Apple, Google, RevenueCat), and carries a card telling the Player to cancel the subscription in the store BEFORE deleting.
- Email path: write from the Account's own address (that is the identity check Google permits), 30 days max, in practice a few days.
